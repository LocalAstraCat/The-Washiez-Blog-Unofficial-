# Washiez Brand Reference Notes

The redesign is an original Chronicle interface. It must not reproduce the official Washiez website’s layout, typography, images, graphic treatment, or other visual arrangements.

The user-approved palette cues are inferred from the supplied Washiez logo and game imagery: **vivid red**, **royal blue**, **lime green**, **sunny yellow**, **sky blue**, **white foam**, and **charcoal outlines**. They will inform an original, energetic car-wash editorial system with clear content hierarchy and accessible contrast.

The official Washiez site may be consulted only for factual game information and color confirmation. No official-site visuals are used in the Chronicle.
